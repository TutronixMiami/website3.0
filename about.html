Andrew Senorans / Founder’s story

I built the support I needed.

I founded Tutronix to be the kind of academic support I wish I had growing up. When a capable student is struggling, the answer is not always another explanation. Sometimes they need the tools to plan, begin, follow through, and ultimately succeed on their own.

Engineer → Tutor → Founder
My story / Keep reading ↓
Chapter01

Smart. Disruptive. Misunderstood.

As a kid, I struggled with anxiety and ADHD. I would zone out during lectures, constantly interrupt, and was known as a troublemaker. I was smart, but disruptive, and no one knew what to make of that.

At one point, I was tested for autism and cycled through therapists and psychologists. Teachers did not know whether to be impressed or concerned. I was not failing classes, but I was not exactly thriving either. Honestly, it is shocking that my grades were as high as they were, considering how little I could focus.

The way I learned and functioned did not match what the system expected from a “good student.”

I was a gifted child falling behind in a system that did not understand how I learned.
The placement test / Fifth grade02

Then I passed the test I was meant to fail.

In fifth grade, I accidentally aced a math placement test that most students were meant to fail. The school sent me upstairs, alone, to sit in a middle school prealgebra class with older kids.

Math placement / Grade 5Unexpected result
ACED.

No explanation. No support. No credit.

“What do we do with him?”

I was the youngest person in the room, and I kept up just fine. But it was clear: the school did not know what to do with me. Again.

The person who saw what others missed

My mom
listened.

She saw my love of science and enrolled me in a math and science program for grades 6–12. That decision gave my curiosity somewhere to go.

Behind the grades03

I was succeeding on paper. Behind the scenes was chaos.

In middle and high school, I began to shine, especially in math and science. But my grades sometimes dropped for reasons that had nothing to do with comprehension. Anxiety, ADHD, and focus issues would creep back in.

MISSING / 01A forgotten assignment.
LOST / 02A project slipping out of view.
STUCK / 03No clear place to begin.

No one taught me how to manage my time. No one showed me how to stay organized, initiate tasks, or remain focused when my mind was racing. That lingering chaos never fully went away, and I carried the frustration quietly for years.

University of Miami / Mechanical engineering04

Structure changed what intelligence could do.

College brought a new level of responsibility: complex schedules, long term projects, relationships, deadlines, and motivation without anyone looking over my shoulder.

I learned to build systems that worked for me, not because anyone taught me how, but because I had to. Slowly, I developed the executive function skills I had never been explicitly taught as a kid.

My earlier struggle had little to do with intelligence. It had everything to do with structure.
The realization that became Tutronix

The student understood the work. The grade still did not move.

Inside the tutoring hour

“I get it.”

During college, I began tutoring math and science. I would break down the material, concepts clicked, and students completed problems independently. They were capable. They could do the work.

Across the rest of the week

Still not turned in.

A week later, assignments were missing, test scores had not improved, grades stayed low, and confidence stayed lower. Better explanations alone were not enough.

This was not only a content problem.
It was an executive function problem.

Tutronix today05

Where learning meets life.

That realization inspired me to build Tutronix: a place where students do not just learn better. They function better. Expert tutoring is combined with coaching in focus, organization, planning, and mindset: the skills that move the needle for long term success.
