01 / The real distinction

Tutoring helps students understand the work.

Tutronix also helps them manage everything around it—and follow through.

REGULAR TUTORING ASKS

“Does the student understand this assignment?”

TUTRONIX ALSO ASKS

“Can they see the week, choose a starting point, begin, stay with it, and adjust when the plan changes?”

THE RESULT

Subject understanding becomes part of a larger system the student can increasingly direct for themselves.

02 / Three tools, one workbench

Support designed to work together.

These are not three disconnected services. Each one acts on the same student, the same assignments, and the same week—at a different level.

One-to-one tutoring

Magnify the learning.
Find where understanding breaks down, slow the subject down, and build real confidence.

Academic coaching

Map the direction.
See the whole landscape, set priorities, and turn competing obligations into a usable plan.

Executive function

Activate the plan.
Build practical systems for time, task initiation, organization, flexibility, and follow-through.
